About
Matt

Designer, creative director, and craft obsessive. I’ve been shaping brands and experiences for over a decade.

M

Based in the US, I’ve spent over a decade at the intersection of strategy and aesthetics — helping brands find their voice, define their presence, and connect with the people who matter most to them.

My work spans brand identity, UX/UI design, editorial art direction, and design systems. Whether I’m building a visual language from scratch or refining an existing one, I approach every project with the same commitment: rigor, curiosity, and an uncompromising attention to detail.

Before going independent, I worked at leading design studios and consultancies, collaborating with clients across technology, architecture, media, and consumer goods — from early-stage startups finding their footing to established institutions undergoing transformation.

Outside of work, you’ll find me in bookstores, on trails, or obsessively reorganizing my type specimen collection.

What I Believe

01

Clarity over complexity

Simple solutions take more work, and they’re almost always better. I resist the temptation to over-design.

02

Context first

Great design is inseparable from its context. I start every project by asking why before asking what.

03

Craft matters

The details most people never consciously notice are often the ones that create the feeling they can’t forget.
